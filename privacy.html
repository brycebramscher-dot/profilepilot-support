Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

ProfilePilot provides AI-powered feedback designed to help users improve their dating profiles. This Privacy Policy explains what information ProfilePilot collects, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

ProfilePilot may collect information you provide when using the app, including:

  • Email address and account information.
  • Photos you choose to submit for profile analysis.
  • Dating profile text, prompts, answers, bios, and other content you choose to submit.
  • User and account identifiers needed to operate your account.
  • Purchase and subscription entitlement information associated with ProfilePilot.
  • Basic usage information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Provide AI-powered dating profile feedback and analysis.
  • Authenticate and maintain user accounts.
  • Manage free analyses, purchased analyses, and subscription entitlements.
  • Restore eligible purchases and subscriptions.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of ProfilePilot.
  • Troubleshoot problems and improve the service.

AI Processing

Content you submit for analysis, which may include photos and profile text, is processed using artificial intelligence services in order to generate your requested ProfilePilot feedback. Information is sent only as necessary to provide the analysis you request.

Service Providers

ProfilePilot uses third-party service providers to operate the app. These may include Supabase for account and backend services, OpenAI for AI-powered analysis, Apple for App Store purchases and subscriptions, and hosting infrastructure used to operate ProfilePilot's backend services.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services to ProfilePilot and are subject to their respective privacy and security practices.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple. ProfilePilot receives transaction and entitlement information needed to verify purchases and provide access to purchased features. ProfilePilot does not receive your full payment-card information from Apple.

Data Retention

ProfilePilot retains account and service information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain account and purchase entitlements, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the security of the service.

Content submitted for AI analysis is processed as necessary to provide the requested analysis. Retention by third-party service providers may also be governed by their applicable policies and service terms.

Data Sharing

ProfilePilot does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate ProfilePilot, provide requested AI analysis, process or verify purchases, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your ProfilePilot account and associated personal information by contacting us at the email address below. Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record, or other legitimate operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

ProfilePilot is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through ProfilePilot. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ProfilePilot changes. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
